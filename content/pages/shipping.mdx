## Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 on a weekday leave the same day from our warehouse outside Gothenburg.

| Market | Carrier | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sweden | PostNord | 1–2 days | Free over 500 SEK |
| Norway | PostNord | 2–4 days | Free over 500 NOK |
| Denmark | PostNord | 2–3 days | Free over 400 DKK |

## Returns

Thirty days, unworn, tags on. The return label is in the box — you do not need to ask us for one.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within five working days of the parcel reaching us.
