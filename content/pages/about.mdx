We started Acme because buying a plain t-shirt had become unaccountably difficult. Everything was
either disposable or priced as though the fabric had been flown in by helicopter.

So we make a short list of things, in heavy cotton, in Portugal, and we do not change the list very
often. When something is good we keep making it. When it is not, we stop.

## How we price

The price is the price. We do not run a permanent sale against an invented list price, and we do
not discount at the end of a season — we would rather make fewer of a thing and sell them all.

We ship from Gothenburg.
